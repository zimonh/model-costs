How do I find out when an LLM API price changes?

apicost.app lists every change in the LiteLLM price list on this page, with its date. Follow the Atom feed at https://apicost.app/pricing/changes/feed.xml in any feed reader, or download https://apicost.app/price-history.json. The list can lag a provider's announcement by a few days, so for news from today, check the provider's own pricing page.
