Questions and answers

What is a batch API?

A batch API lets you send many requests at once as a file. The provider processes them within 24 hours, when it has spare capacity, and charges less per token than for real-time requests.

How much cheaper is the batch API?

53 models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini and xAI offer a batch API: you submit requests in bulk, they are processed within 24 hours, and you pay less, typically 50% less than the standard price.
